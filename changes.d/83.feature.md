`xdiff plot -o` draws a new difference map: land in grey with a coastline traced from the data's sea mask (land was white before), degree axes, a horizontal colour bar, the variable's long name with the real date and depth, and a footer with min, max, RMS and the share of points that differ. The colour scale is linear, or a signed log scale when the differences span many orders of magnitude; `--scale auto|linear|log` overrides the automatic choice.
